Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to keeping your data minimal, secure, and under your control.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address, which are used to identify you and allow you to sign in. When you use Spendly, you voluntarily provide expense records including amount, category, date, and an optional description.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as basic device and browser metadata, to help us diagnose issues and improve reliability. We do not collect your location, contacts, or financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information you provide is used to operate Spendly: authenticating you, storing and displaying your expenses, generating category breakdowns and date-range summaries, and remembering your preferences. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example, total number of users) may be used to understand usage trends, but these statistics contain no personal details.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and is associated with your account. We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Access to personal data is restricted to those who need it to operate and improve the service.

You may update or delete individual expense records at any time from within the application. You may also request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us using the details below.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third party providers to deliver the service, such as hosting, database, and email delivery services. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their function and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

Spendly does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics scripts that track you across other sites, or social media platforms. We do not share your data with marketers or data brokers.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct inaccurate information, and to request deletion of your account and associated records. You can exercise many of these rights directly from your account settings. For anything else, please reach out using the contact details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are material, we will provide a more visible notice within the application. Continued use of Spendly after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the details provided in the application or on our website. We will do our best to respond in a timely manner.
